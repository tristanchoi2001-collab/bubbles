## 영문 초안

**¶1 — Problem.** Gas–liquid flow in gaps of a fraction of a millimetre to a few millimetres between flat plates occurs in plate heat exchangers, electronics cold plates, electrochemical cells and the coolant gaps of compact reactors. Its frictional pressure drop is usually predicted with Lockhart–Martinelli-type correlations whose Chisholm parameter C is fitted to flow rates and hydraulic diameter (e.g. Mishima & Hibiki 1996; Lee & Lee 2001), and its void fraction with a drift-flux distribution parameter close to C₀ ≈ 1.35 for narrow rectangular ducts (Ishii 1977; Mishima et al. 1993). Neither description contains the size or shape of the bubbles.

**¶2 — Why bubble size should matter.** When bubbles are larger than the gap they become pancakes whose depth-averaged motion is that of an inclusion in Hele-Shaw (Darcy) flow. Their speed relative to the liquid depends on their size and on the capillary number through the thin films on the walls (Taylor & Saffman 1959; Kopf-Sill & Homsy 1988; Booth et al. 2023), and their shape changes with a gap-scale Reynolds number (Moriyama & Inoue 1993; Monnet et al. 2022). A bubble that lags the liquid acts as an obstacle and a bubble that outruns it acts as a sink of resistance, so at the same flow rates the pressure drop should depend on how the gas is split into bubbles.

**¶3 — What has been done.** Measured void fractions have been used to predict the pressure drop in narrow channels before. Sadatomi et al. (1992) found that a separated-flow model, in which the liquid flows alone at its interstitial velocity j_L/(1 − α) with single-phase friction (Chierici et al. 1974), reproduced their data for 0.78- and 1.47-mm gaps best. Iida & Takahashi (1976) measured pressure drop and photographic void fraction in horizontal gaps of 0.7–10 mm and found α ≈ 0.75β; Kumagai et al. (2008) mapped flow regimes across 0.5–10 mm. In gaps of tens of micrometres the two-phase multiplier collapsed to Chisholm's C ≈ 0 (Moriyama et al. 1992), whereas in a 1-mm smooth fracture at higher liquid velocities the relative permeability depended on the flow rates themselves (Fourar et al. 1993). These observations have not been brought under one description, and none of these studies measured the kinematics of individual bubbles together with the pressure drop.

**¶4 — This work.** We treat gap-spanning bubbles as two-dimensional inclusions and obtain, to the order of mean-field theory, E = φ_L² − 1 = c·α/(1 − α) − (c − 1)·B, where B = j_G/j_L and the shape factor c = 1 + C_A is set by the potential-flow added-mass coefficient C_A of the bubble footprint (c = 2 for circles, 1 + b/a for aligned ellipses). The separated-flow model is the dipole-free limit c = 1. Plotted as y = E(1 − α)/α against the mobility deficit x = 1 − s̄, s̄ = B(1 − α)/α, every model becomes a straight line through (0, 1), so changing only the injector at fixed flow rates discriminates them by the sign of the slope, a test that errors in the imaged void fraction cannot mimic. We test the closure in horizontal gaps of 0.3, 0.7 and 1 mm with deep-learning detection and tracking of [N] bubbles, synchronised differential pressure, gas step responses that measure the gas fraction without a flowmeter, and injectors of different size, and we map where it fails as inertia grows, using Λ = Re_H·H/(10d), and as bubbles stop spanning the gap at 3 and 7 mm.

## 한국어 요지 (심사자 관점 점검)
- ¶1~2: 상관식(C, C₀)에 기포 크기·모양이 없다는 문제와, Hele-Shaw에서는 크기·모양이 속도를 정한다는 근거.
- ¶3: **선행을 먼저 인정한다.** 측정 α로 ΔP(Sadatomi 1992), 사진 기공률 + ΔP의 다중 갭(Iida 1976), 양식 지도(Kumagai 2008), Darcy 영역 C ≈ 0(Moriyama 1992), 관성 영역 유량 의존(Fourar 1993). 빈칸은 "한 설명으로 묶이지 않았다"와 "기포별 운동학 + ΔP를 함께 잰 연구가 없다".
- ¶4: 새것 = 모양 가족 폐합(부가질량 C_A), 분리류 = c = 1 극한, 판별 평면과 기울기 부호, 계단 응답, Λ에 따른 전이.
- 위험: ¶4의 "errors cannot mimic"은 E > 0일 때만 맞다(E ≈ 0 근처에서는 α 오차 방향이 거의 수평). 독립 점검(review_3) 결과에 따라 고친다. Chierici 1974, Ishii 1977은 2차 인용 수준(원문 미확인) — 투고 전 원문 확인.
